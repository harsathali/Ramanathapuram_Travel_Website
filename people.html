BORN IN RAMESWARAM

A.P.J. ABDUL KALAM

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam (15 October 1931 – 27 July 2015)

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping; it is something that does not let you sleep."

Born on 15 October 1931 in the island town of Rameswaram into a Tamil Muslim marakkayar family, Dr. Kalam rose from modest beginnings delivering newspapers along the coastal railway tracks to become India’s supreme missile scientist, aerospace visionary, and the beloved 11th President of India (2002–2007).

Throughout his illustrious life, his bond with Rameswaram remained unbroken. He credited his deep spiritual pluralism and scientific curiosity to the inclusive seaside ethos of Rameswaram, where the temple priests and mosque imams shared meals and philosophical dialogues.

1931 — Rameswaram Childhood

Raised on Mosque Street, Rameswaram. Attended Schwartz Higher Secondary School in Ramanathapuram, walking miles daily with a fierce thirst for knowledge.

1969–1982 — ISRO & SLV-III Triumph

Served as Project Director of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), successfully deploying the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

1982–1999 — Missile Pioneer & Pokhran-II

Chief of DRDO, spearheading the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (Agni and Prithvi missiles), earning the title "Missile Man of India".

2002–2007 — 11th President of India

Unanimously elected as India’s President. Re-opened Rashtrapati Bhavan to students, youths, and farmers, forever immortalized as the "People's President".

2017 — Pei Karumbu National Memorial

Laid to eternal rest at Pei Karumbu in his beloved Rameswaram, where the grand National Memorial now stands as an international monument of inspiration.

BORN IN PARAMAKUDI

KAMAL HAASAN

Born 7 November 1954 in Paramakudi, Ramanathapuram District

"Cinema is not just entertainment; it is the mirror of society, a canvas where courage and art collide."

Born in Paramakudi into the family of criminal lawyer D. Srinivasan, Kamal Haasan’s extraordinary artistic journey began right on the banks of the Vaigai River. Beginning as a child artist at the age of six in Kalathur Kannamma (1960)—which won him the President's Gold Medal—he went on to redefine Indian cinema across six decades.

Actor, director, screenwriter, classical Bharatanatyam dancer, playback singer, and lyricist, Kamal Haasan has won 4 National Film Awards, 19 Filmfare Awards, and holds the historic distinction of having submitted the highest number of films (seven) by an Indian actor for Academy Award consideration.

1954 — Paramakudi Roots

Born in Paramakudi, Ramanathapuram. The town’s rich linguistic nuances and artistic warmth deeply influenced his storytelling voice and cinematic realism.

1960 — Child Prodigy & President's Gold Medal

Made his screen debut in Kalathur Kannamma at age six, instantly recognized by President Dr. S. Radhakrishnan for his naturalistic emotional acting.

1970s–1980s — The Golden Auteur Era

Collaborated with legendary filmmaker K. Balachander. Delivered pathbreaking masterworks like 16 Vayathinile, Moondram Pirai (National Award), and Mani Ratnam’s magnum opus Nayakan (featured in TIME magazine’s All-Time 100 Best Films).

1990s–2000s — Directorial Mastery & Innovation

Wrote and directed cinema milestones including Hey Ram, Virumaandi, and Dasavathaaram (portraying ten distinct characters), pioneering sync sound, live orchestra scores, and advanced prosthetics in Indian cinema.

Present — Global Cinematic Icon

Honoured with the Padma Shri (1990), Padma Bhushan (2014), and France’s prestigious Chevalier de l'Ordre des Arts et des Lettres (2016). Continues to shatter cinematic boundaries with global blockbusters.
